Each graph sweeps one input while holding the other two at their setpoints. A dot marks predicted equilibrium; a cross marks the live state. Hover, touch, or use arrow keys on a graph to inspect the sweep.

Power accounting

Pinput = VₐIₐ + VꜰIꜰ; Pout = Tʟω. Losses are Iₐ²Rₐ, Bω² and field input VꜰIꜰ. The remaining power changes stored rotor energy ½Jω². Total efficiency is Pout / Pinput, shown only near steady motoring with positive useful output.

Model assumptions

Ideal field-current regulation, constant resistance and inertia, no inductance, saturation, brush drop or thermal dynamics. The connected armature supply can absorb regenerative power. Reducing voltage can make armature current and torque negative; at a zero-volt setpoint, the connected armature dynamically brakes through its resistance. Disconnect or emergency stop instead opens the circuit. A passive load resists forward motion and holds the shaft at zero rather than driving it backward. Stalled torque is balanced by a load reaction. Open-circuit armature current is zero; the field remains energized.

Midpoint integration uses steps no larger than 1/240 s. Frames are bounded to 0.1 s; a hidden tab pauses simulation time. Rotation is smoothly integrated at 1:40 visual scale. Reduced-motion preferences suppress decorative movement, with all readings still live.

05 Operating envelope+
!

Overspeed > 4,000 RPM

Reduce voltage or increase field current. Weakening the field can raise equilibrium speed.

!

Excess current |Iₐ| > 60 A

Includes startup and regenerative current. Reduce voltage or load as appropriate and allow transients to settle.

!

Stall Insufficient starting torque

At rest, Tₑ ≤ Tʟ with armature voltage applied. Reduce load or increase available starting torque.

!

Field weakening Iꜰ < 0.50 A

Less flux means less torque per ampere. Watch both speed and current.

Emergency stop opens the armature circuit.

The shaft coasts under friction and the selected load; field excitation remains on. Release the stop latch, then explicitly energize to restart. Escape always activates the stop.

Illustrative lab warning thresholds, not manufacturer ratings. Warnings do not automatically trip the motor. This model is an educational simulation.
